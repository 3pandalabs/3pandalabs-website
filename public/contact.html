Questions about a charge

Card charges from our apps appear on your statement as 3PANDALABS, usually followed by the app's name (for example 3PANDALABS* TRANSITVAU). If you don't recognise one, email us before contacting your bank and include:

  • the date and amount of the charge,
  • the last four digits of the card, and
  • the email address you used with the app, if you know it.

We never ask for your full card number or password by email.
