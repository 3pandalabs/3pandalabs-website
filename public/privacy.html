Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

This page covers 3pandalabs.com. Each 3PandaLabs app has its own privacy policy describing exactly what that app collects and why; those are linked below.

This website

  • No accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or ads. The site is a few static pages.
  • Hosting. The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and standard request details to deliver and protect it.
  • Fonts. Pages load the Geist font from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when you visit.
  • Email. If you email us, we keep the message and your address to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. We don't sell it or add you to a mailing list.

Every other 3PandaLabs app links its own terms and privacy policy inside the app and on its checkout pages.

Payments

Card payments in our apps are handled by Stripe, which receives your card details directly. We receive the payment result, the amount and limited card details (such as the brand and last four digits), never the full card number.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email 3pandas@3pandalabs.com. For data held by a specific app, that app's privacy policy and its settings page explain the options, including deleting your account.
