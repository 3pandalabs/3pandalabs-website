Refunds and cancellations

Last updated 17 November 2026

This is our general refund policy. Each app's terms of service set out the details for that app's products, and they apply where they are more specific.

Monthly plans

  • Cancel at any time from the app; no contract and no cancellation fee.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep the plan until the end of the period you've paid for. We don't pro-rate a partial month.

One-off purchases

  • Not delivered yet: full refund if you ask before we deliver it.
  • We couldn't deliver it: full refund, or the credit back, without you having to ask.
  • Delivered: not refundable, because it was made for you. Many apps let you redo or adjust what you bought instead; see the app.

Mistakes

If you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or charged for something you didn't order, we refund it in full. Tell us within 60 days of the charge.

How to ask

Email 3pandas@3pandalabs.com with the date and amount of the charge and the email you used in the app. Approved card refunds go back to the original card; banks usually show them within 5 to 10 business days.

Nothing here limits any refund right you have under laws that can't be excluded.
